# VILDRUM Research Findings

## Reference Site: Vipp.com

### Design Language Observations
- **Navigation**: Minimal top nav — logo left (lowercase sans-serif), menu items center (Products, Kitchens, Guesthouses, World of Vipp, Professionals), search + cart icons right. Transparent over hero, solid on scroll.
- **Hero**: Full-width immersive photography with text overlay centered. Large, thin headline typography. Subtle CTA as text link with arrow. No buttons.
- **Product Sections**: Clean grids with editorial photography on neutral backgrounds. Product names in small, tracked-out uppercase. Generous whitespace.
- **Typography**: Sans-serif (appears to be a clean geometric sans), generous letter-spacing on headlines, refined weight hierarchy (thin/light for headlines, regular for body).
- **Color Palette**: Near-black (#1a1a1a), warm whites, subtle greys. Very muted, warm neutral palette. No saturated colors.
- **CTAs**: Understated text links with small arrows — never bold buttons. Black underline on hover.
- **Spacing**: Massive whitespace between sections. Breathing room is a design element.
- **Footer**: Multi-column — Company links, Customer service, Professionals, Newsletter signup with email input and country selector.
- **Overall Feel**: Ultra-premium, editorial, architectural, confident minimalism. Every element earns its place.

## Competitive Landscape

### Inflatable Shelter Market
- **AirNEST (Canada)**: Push-button inflatable, ~$1,349, focuses on convenience and speed. Blue/orange sporty aesthetic. Technical specs heavy.
- **Coody (South Korea)**: Premium inflatable tents, 72-hour pressure testing, modular design, stove jack for winter. Range from ~$800-$3,000. Dark/technical aesthetic.
- **Air Architecture**: House-shaped inflatable, white "cloud" aesthetic, 8-minute setup via hand pump. More playful/modern.
- **Heimplanet**: German brand with geodesic inflatable tents. Technical, expedition-focused. Higher price point.

### Brand Positioning Gap
No brand occupies the **design-conscious, architectural, Scandinavian** space for inflatable shelters. Most competitors focus on technical specs or outdoor adventure aesthetics. VILDRUM fills the gap between "camping tent" and "architectural shelter."

## Scandinavian Outdoor Aesthetic References
- **Vipp Shelter**: Black steel prefab cabin, 55 sqm, floor-to-ceiling glass, minimalist interior. €1,000/night. The gold standard.
- **Nordic cabin culture**: "Hytte" (Norway) / "Stuga" (Sweden) — simplicity, natural materials, large windows, connection to landscape.
- **Key elements**: Natural light as design material, wood/fire, wool/linen textures, muted earth tones, absence of clutter.
- **Color palette**: Limestone, warm sand, pine green, charcoal, fog grey, warm white — never cold or clinical.
